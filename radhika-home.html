Project Overview

Welcome to Radhika Home — a thoughtfully designed premium duplex residential project by Swarna Bhumi Developers, located in the fast-growing locality of Rangabazar. Architecturally designed by Elegant Architects, Radhika Home offers spacious and modern 3 BHK and 4 BHK duplex villas starting at an affordable ₹55 Lakhs.
